# DOX.md — Reusable templates

## Purpose

Own portable documents copied into an adopting project.

## Ownership

- root-DOX.md becomes the adopting project's root DOX.md.
- child-DOX.md becomes a scoped child DOX.md where a durable boundary needs one.
- beads-AGENTS.fragment.md is an optional entry-point addition for initialized Beads workspaces.

## Local Contracts

- These are distribution templates, not this repository's own project contracts.
- Keep root and child templates free of project-specific paths, sample verification claims and generated indexes.
- Leave unknown Work Guidance and Verification empty; fill them from the adopting project's actual instructions and checks.
- Do not copy the Beads fragment into projects without an initialized Beads workspace.

## Work Guidance

Keep templates concise and preserve the six-section document shape.

## Verification

Check README.md points to the copyable templates. Verify placeholders require project discovery and do not claim that an unknown project has no child documents.

## Child DOX Index

- None. These three template files remain owned here.
